Questions & Support

Can I deploy on-premise? add
Yes, ModelWise offers a self-hosted Docker orchestration engine for enterprise teams who require complete control over their inference traffic and keys.
How do you handle research data? add
Our transit layer is stateless. We use encrypted memory buffers to route prompts directly to providers. No metadata or body content is ever persisted without explicit consent.
What is the latency overhead? add
The ModelWise routing overhead is typically less than 0.05ms, making it virtually transparent even in high-throughput production environments.
